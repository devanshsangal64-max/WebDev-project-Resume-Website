About me

I'm a 19-year-old undergraduate pursuing two degrees side by side. I was unsure whether to commit to law or business, so I chose to study both: a BA in Legal Studies and a BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship.

Career goal: I want to gain solid work experience first, then start my own business and become financially independent. I'm resilient and competitive, and I don't leave a task until it's done properly.

After Class 12, I took a drop year to prepare for CLAT. I didn't get the result I wanted, but the effort earned me a 75% scholarship at Jindal. That year taught me how to keep going when things don't work out.
